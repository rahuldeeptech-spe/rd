      Keep ScamCheck free

      This tool has no ads and no tracking. If it saved you or your family from a scam, a small contribution pays for development and hosting.

      This is a static, rule-based audit. It cannot see what a server does at runtime, and it can both miss attacks and flag harmless text. Treat a clean result as "no known red flags", never as proof of safety. Read a server's source before you trust it with your files or tokens.
